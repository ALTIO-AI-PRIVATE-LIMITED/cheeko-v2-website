Where Cheeko is not the right pick.

Then a plain audio-only story box is the honest choice, not Cheeko. Our screen shows a still picture and stops, but it is a screen. If your family rule is no pixels at all, respect. Buy the box.

Your child is under 3

Cheeko is built for ages 3–10. Under 3, kids need faces, floors and hands, not devices, ours included. Wait for the third birthday; Cheeko will still be here.

You never have Wi-Fi at all

A card downloads once on Wi-Fi, then plays offline forever. But that first download does need Wi-Fi: a home network, a hotspot, a relative's house, anything. Truly zero Wi-Fi, ever? New cards can't load.
