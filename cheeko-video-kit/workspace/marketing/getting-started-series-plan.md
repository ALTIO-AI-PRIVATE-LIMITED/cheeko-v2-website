## The idea

One story told in 4 short parts. Each part works alone as a Reel (45 to 60 s), and all 4 stitch into one film (about 3.5 minutes) for YouTube, the website's "How it works" page and WhatsApp for new buyers. We build the parts one by one, then re-render everything together at the end with the same music and style so it plays as one film.

| Part | ID | Title | For | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | V20 | Meet Cheeko | Everyone (intro) | ~50 s |
| 2 | V21 | Set Up Cheeko | Parents | ~60 s |
| 3 | V22 | Learn to Play | Kids (and parents watching) | ~45 s |
| 4 | V23 | Your First Five Minutes | Kids and parents | ~50 s |
| All | V24 | Getting Started with Cheeko (full film) | YouTube, website, WhatsApp | ~3.5 min, vertical and 16:9 |

Each part gets English and Hindi versions and a thumbnail. Each part ends with a "Part N of 4" slate that points to the next one; the full film uses chapter cards instead.

## Style

- Bright Cheeko brand look (like the feature videos), but calmer and clearer, because people follow along: one step on screen at a time, big step numbers ("Step 1 of 5"), short labels, nothing flashes past before it can be read.
- Parts 1 and 2 use the narrator (English_Upbeat_Woman, calm and happy). Part 3 uses Cheeko's REAL voice recorded on the device (the first-boot tutorial lines). Part 4 mixes both.
- Every device screen is the real firmware screen; every app screen is the real app rendered with sample data (the same method as V19).
- Music: one calm theme that runs through all four parts, so the full film feels like one piece.

## What the device really does on first boot (checked in firmware 2.4.311, 2026-09-29)

Switch on (power switch on the side) -> no Wi-Fi saved, so the screen asks "WI-FI? CONNECT NOW" -> Connect -> "Getting ready" (restarts into phone setup) -> the parent connects it from the app over Bluetooth and picks the home Wi-Fi -> it checks for updates -> it speaks and shows a 6-digit pairing code -> the parent types it in the app -> Cheeko says "Hello friend, your Cheeko is ready. Pop in a card and let's begin our adventure together." -> the first-boot tutorial -> play.

Cheeko's real recorded tutorial lines (from the device): "Hello, I am Cheeko. Let's learn to play." / "Turn the knob to look around." / "Now press it to choose." / "Press two times to go back." / "Tap the screen to pick things." / "Swipe up to go home." / "Put a card on me." / "That's it. Cards give me new things to say." / "You're ready. Let's play."

## Open questions for Ravi

1. **Turning it on (answered 2026-09-29):** there is a physical ON/OFF power switch on the side (Ravi; firmware: "The physical ON/OFF switch remains the manual power control"). When idle the screen dims after 2 minutes, turns off at 5, and after 30 minutes Cheeko naps; pressing the knob wakes it.
2. **What's in the box:** the tender sheet says device, 10 cards, lanyard, quick-start guide and a USB-C cable; the website says no charger. Is the cable in the box? Is there a lanyard and a guide?
3. **Card slot:** on top? (The website's labelled photo says "at the back", but the arrow points to the top, and the tutorial says "Put a card on me".)
4. **The pairing voice line needs fixing:** when it shows the code, the device says "Please log into the control panel to add the device, enter the verification code." That is a leftover generic line and sounds wrong for a kids' product. I suggest re-recording it, for example "Open the Cheeko app and type this code." Until then the video mutes that line and the narrator explains.
5. **Order on first boot:** I read it from the firmware (Wi-Fi, update, code, "your Cheeko is ready", tutorial). Worth one check on a real unit.
6. **Hindi versions:** Cheeko's tutorial recordings on the device are English only, so the Hindi parts would have a Hindi narrator with Cheeko speaking English (as the real device does). OK?
7. **Real footage (optional):** 10 seconds of hands unboxing and plugging in USB-C would make Parts 1 and 2 feel real. Without it we use product photos.
