## Never show
- "Hey Cheeko" voice wake (no wake word; press the knob). LEDs (none).
- Talk / Imagine / Radio working offline.
- Disabled games: Shapes, Math, Race, Tetris, Maze, Tic Tac Toe (README and tender still list some).
- Unreachable app screens: chat transcripts, character selection, screen-time limits; no subscription screen.
- Age-band behaviour until it's on prod (dev only as of 2026-09-28).
- Bujho, Ginti, Tikku, Vanya (backend only, not in the device's Talk carousel).
- Certifications as done (BIS/WPC/EMC in progress).
- The word "once" / "one-time" with the price; "Ships by 25 September".
