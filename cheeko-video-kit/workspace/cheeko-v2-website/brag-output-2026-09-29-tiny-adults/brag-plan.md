# V09 Tiny Adults: script and shot list

2D sticker ad, Instagram Reel, 1080x1920, 43.9 s. At a 7-year-old's birthday party, phone-raised kids behave like tiny adults. Freeze, rewind, and Cheeko turns them back into kids.

- **Voices (MiniMax speech-2.8-hd, one clip per line with its own emotion):** narrator `English_Upbeat_Woman` at 1.1x. Kids at 1.12x: Arjun (Corporate) `English_Strong-WilledBoy`, Myra (Influencer) `English_PlayfulGirl`, Anaya (News) `English_UpsetGirl`, Bunty (WhatsApp Uncle) `English_SadTeen`, Pihu (Burnt out) `English_Soft-spokenGirl`. The Funny Voice chipmunk is Bunty's own line, pitched up.
- **Music:** Envato Elements "Quirky Fun Sports Party Dance" by SunChannelMusic, downloaded by Ravi 2026-09-29 and licensed to his account. It follows the story:
  - Loud chorus on "birthday party!"
  - A record scratch cuts it.
  - Bass, organ and soft drums stems only under the grown-up scenes.
  - Silence on the freeze.
  - The full final chorus when Cheeko lands, placed so the song's final hit falls on the end card.
- **Sound effects:** real Cheeko sounds (boot, low battery, popup, success, knob click) plus synthesised party blower, record scratch, phone ring, shutter, news sting, stamps, tape stop, rewind and boings.
- **Art:** Ravi's ChatGPT stickers. The 14-sticker sheet is cut by `work/cut_stickers.py`, and 7 single high-res stickers in `stickers/hires-source/` replace their sheet versions. Real device with real firmware screens (Talk listening, Funny Voice Chipmunk, Jump game) and the 10 real cards.
