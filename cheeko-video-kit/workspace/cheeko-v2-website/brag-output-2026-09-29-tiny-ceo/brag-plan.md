# V08 Tiny CEO: script and shot list

Original concept informed by research into Moonshot's comedy mechanics, not a recreation of a Moonshot advertisement. Intended viewer: Indian parents. Product user: child age 3-10. Problem illustrated: handing over a parent's phone also exposes a child to a feed designed around adult interests. We make no medical, developmental or guaranteed-outcome claims.

## Research
- Moonshot's official Swiggy Instamart / Juhi Chawla campaign listing: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qCtuyuKFpnw
- Devaiah Bopanna's first-party campaign credits: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/devaiah-bopanna-6a50b716_marketing-content-activity-7242862386540363776-mV6l
- May 2026 interview with Bopanna and Hocco: https://marketingmind.in/how-hocco-and-moonshot-made-ice-cream-advertising-lose-the-plot/
- July 2026 Bopanna interview: https://marketingmind.in/if-people-remember-the-joke-theyll-remember-the-brand-moonshots-devaiah-bopanna/

My synthesis: a familiar situation treated with absurd seriousness; expectation reversal; escalating jokes; everyday Indian context; product truth resolving the absurdity; final callback. Interview/campaign pages reviewed, not a claim to have watched inaccessible full films. No Moonshot dialogue, campaign footage or celebrity likeness used.
