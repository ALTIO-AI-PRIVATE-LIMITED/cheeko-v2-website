# V06 Five Minutes: script and shot list

2D sticker ad, Instagram Reel, 1080x1920, 34.4 s. Quirky and sarcastic: the family phone narrates its own downfall.

- **Voice:** the phone is MiniMax speech-2.8-hd `English_Upbeat_Woman`, played 8% faster, one clip per line with its own emotion. Nani is `hindi_female_2_v1` (the English voices say "Arre" as "array").
- **Music:** Envato Elements "Sneaky Pizzicato Comedy" by Korolkov (Lifetime Commercial License, CFT360 Design Studio workspace, downloaded 2026-09-29). It starts 48.0 s into the track so the track's own ending lands on the end card.
- **Sound effects:** real Cheeko sounds (the doorbell and clock from the Sounds Around Me card, the device's low-battery, charge-full, card-insert, success and popup sounds) plus synthesised pops, stamps and a sad trombone.
- **Art:** Ravi's family storyboard panels 01 to 12 (cut out by `work/cut_panels.py`) and his home sticker library (kid-happy, family-playing-together, home-door, home-fridge). The device, Nani, the fox and the 10 cards are the real assets. The device always shows real firmware screens.
- **Left out on purpose:** storyboard panels 13 to 16 (an AI-drawn device, and the card goes in at the top, which is unsettled) and `device_live.png` (old UI).
