# /brag plan: Cheeko manifesto (#1 on marketing/cheeko-video-ideas.md)

**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30fps · **Tone:** warm, emotional, then playful · **Length:** 37.5s · **Voiceover:** Sarvam bulbul:v3, Indian English, speaker "kavya" (alternatives in voice-samples/)

## Angle
The brand film. Every parent knows the phone problem; Cheeko is the screen that knows when to stop. Built on the site's own manifesto lines: "Less screen, more childhood.", "No video. No feed. No ads. All play.", "It's made to end."

## Sound
Original, synthesised in work/synth.py. Dim B-minor pad and clock tick under the phone scenes, a lift into D major at 100 BPM when Cheeko appears, light drums from 11.75, drums out for the calm "made to end" stretch, back for the outro, resolving on D. Music ducks ~8 dB under the voice (VO sits ~12 dB above the bed).
