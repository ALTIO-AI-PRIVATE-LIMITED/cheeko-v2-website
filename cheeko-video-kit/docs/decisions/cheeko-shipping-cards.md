Ravi shared the Drive folder "Cards Creatives" (https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Ys0iuunKGdao239mDC72f_hMbcQbd15A) on 2026-09-28 as "the actual cards that will be shipping". Saved to `Cheeko Master/marketing/cards-shipping/originals/` (+ README) and video-ready 760×1196 JPGs in `cheeko-v2-website/assets/img/cards-shipping/` (large/ for backgrounds; not committed).

The 10: Tales of Kindness (Cheeko × Storynory), Floor is Lava!, Play & Sing Along, Dreamy Melodies, Mitthu the Parrot (Your English Tutor), Make Your Own, Clever Little Tales, The Adventures of Ravi & Nila, Sounds Around Me (art: telephone, bell, drum, car), Nani.

**Why:** the website still shows Storytime Adventures, Ravi's Wild Journey and Around the House (cooker whistle, doorbell) — none of those names ship.

**How to apply:** never use `live/card-*.jpg` or `card_habits.jpg` in videos. The backend's sound-quiz pack was designed as "Around the House" with doorbell + pressure cooker (cheeko-backend docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-11-sound-pack-game-card-design.md); Ravi confirmed on 2026-09-28 that the shipping "Sounds Around Me" card DOES include the pressure cooker and doorbell sounds (its art just shows telephone, bell, drum, car).
