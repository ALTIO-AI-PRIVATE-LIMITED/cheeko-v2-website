Straight answers

Does it need the internet?
Only sometimes. A new card downloads once on Wi-Fi, then plays offline forever. The 8 built-in games and Funny Voice work offline too. Talk, Imagine and Radio need Wi-Fi.
What exactly is the subscription?
Talk (live AI answers) and Imagine (the picture maker) are free for your first 3 months, then part of an optional plan — the price is shown in the parent app before you pay anything. Your cards, stories and games are free forever and never need a plan.
Which languages does it speak?
English plus 10 Indian languages — Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Malayalam and Odia. One card plays in one language, so you pick the language your family speaks.
Is it safe? What does the mic do?
The mic works only while the button is held — Cheeko never listens on its own, and there is no camera. Every AI answer passes child-safety filters, and the parent app shows you every question your child asked.
When does it ship, and what if it breaks?
First orders ship by 8 October 2026, delivery free across India. You get a 6-month warranty and 7-day returns. A human answers on WhatsApp if anything's off.
