# /brag plan: Cheeko cards (single feature)

**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30fps · **Tone:** playful, a story in three acts · **Length:** 22.5s (a bored intro, then 120 BPM from 3.0s)

## Angle

When your kid is bored, don't hand over a smartphone. Hand them Cheeko and a stack of cards. They insert a card and the content plays; swap the card and something new starts. The feature is **the card**.

## Storyboard

| Time | Beat | On screen |

Each old card pops back out and flies away. |

## Sound

The soundtrack is original and synthesised for this video. It opens with a dull, low-passed B-minor pad and a clock tick; the ✕ lands with a buzz, and a riser leads into the drop at 3.0s. From there it's the bright D-major 120 BPM groove (octave bass, marimba skank, glockenspiel hook, kick, claps and hats). Each card round has a whoosh, a thock as it lands, a rising sparkle as the world blooms, and a boing as the card pops out. It resolves on D at 21s.
