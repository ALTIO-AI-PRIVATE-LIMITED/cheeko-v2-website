# V55 Cheeko Store launch: three storylines (2026-10-23)

Made with HyperFrames (HTML rendered to video). The one thing: parents can now pick new learning videos for Cheeko on their phone, and Cheeko learns them over Wi-Fi. The one real thing that travels through every storyline: the **Guess the Dinosaur** video, from its card in the store to Cheeko's screen.

Facts used (from the store code, the live site and Ravi): coming soon, open to everyone, no price, no firmware talk, no games named; the 7 newest videos only: Guess the Instrument (music), Space Quiz (planets and space), Odd One Out (logic), Cheeko's Riddles (thinking), Guess the Dinosaur (dinosaurs), What Is Missing (sharp eyes), Emoji Words (new words). Tagline from the store: "A little more play. A world of learning."

## A. "Done. Done. Done." (recommended)

Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: object-to-object chain (each shot ends on the thing that opens the next).

The boy has finished everything on his Cheeko and is bored; Mumma opens the Cheeko Store on her phone, taps Guess the Dinosaur, and the card itself flies out of the phone into Cheeko's screen. The dinosaur quiz plays full frame, he shouts the answer, and the other six new videos flick past on Cheeko's screen.

- First 2 s: frame 0 is the boy (3D sticker) slumped with the yellow Cheeko; a giant "DONE." stamps three times as Cheeko's real screens flick past (Nani, Quizzy, Mitthu). Voice: "He's finished everything on his Cheeko. Twice!"
- Beats: hook (DONE x3) > tension (Mumma's "Again?" sigh) > reveal (her phone, the real store header lands big: Cheeko Store) > proof: browse (the real video cards scroll, each new one gets its learning word), pick (tap Guess the Dinosaur), send (tap Send to Cheeko Toy, "Sending to Cheeko 40%") > the card lifts out of the phone and arcs into Cheeko ("Hold on! I'm learning new tricks!") > payoff (zoom through Cheeko's screen into the real quiz, "T-Rex!", "Ta-da! I learned new tricks!") > the other six flick past > claim ("A little more play. A world of learning.") > end card (the card carries the Cheeko Store mark in; Coming soon, store.cheekoai.in).
- Look: three worlds: the living room on Cheeko yellow #FFC81A with the sticker family, the phone world on cream #FFF6E8 with the real store UI at about 1.8x, Cheeko's world on ink #231A10 with the real Night screens. Gabarito 900 display, Hanken Grotesk body, orange #F0521D on one word per shot.
- Sound: narrated; our own music bed, quiet and flat during the bored hook, opening up at the store reveal; taps on every touch, a whoosh for the flight, a chime on Ta-da.

## B. "Rewind"

Device: the result first. Form: one continuous take that pulls back.

It opens inside the dinosaur quiz, already playing, with a kid shouting the answer. The camera pulls back to reveal Cheeko's screen, then the kid's lap, then Mumma's phone beside him showing "Sent to Cheeko Toy", then rewinds to how it got there, and rides back out past the six other new videos.

- First 2 s: the real Guess the Dinosaur quiz fills the frame; a kid's voice: "Triceratops!"
- Beats: hook (quiz full frame) > pull back (Cheeko's screen, the kid) > pull back (the phone: Sent) > rewind at speed (the tap, the card, the store home: "Where did that come from?") > ride forward through six stops, one new video each with its learning word > claim > end card.
- Look: the real device videos are the hero; one camera, no cuts; cream ground, Gabarito, ink and yellow.
- Sound: narrated; the bed builds with each pull back and lands on the end card.

## C. "Seven new tricks" (the bold one)

Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.

Giant kinetic type on hard colour planes, cutting on the music: each of the seven new videos slams in as one huge word with the real video playing inside the letters. The phone appears only once, for the one tap that sends it.

- First 2 s: "NEW TRICKS?" fills the frame in Cheeko yellow on ink, the letters punch out to show the dinosaur quiz playing through them. Cheeko: "Hold on! I'm learning new tricks!"
- Beats: hook > seven titles, one per beat, each with its learning word in a colour block > the one UI beat (tap Send) > Cheeko: "Ta-da!" > end card.
- Look: ink #231A10, yellow #FFC81A and orange #F0521D planes at hard edges, Gabarito 900 at about 18% of the frame height, video inside the type.
- Sound: music-led at about 128 BPM, cut on the beat; the narrator only names each video; Cheeko's two lines.

## Assumptions (all storylines)

- 9:16 Reel first, about 35 to 45 s; then a 16:9 version, about 60 s.
- Voice: MiniMax narrator English_Upbeat_Woman, Cheeko in English_PlayfulGirl (about US$0.05 for all lines). Option: Cheeko's real device clips in Chhavi (MSTORE, UPDLEARN, UPDDONE already exist).
- Music: our own synth.py bed (free); or an Envato track if Ravi picks one.
- The store: its real page from its own code, run on the laptop with demo data (a demo parent, one online Cheeko). Nothing in it is redrawn.
