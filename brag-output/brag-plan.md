# /brag plan: Cheeko

**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30fps · **Tone:** polished (serious, elegant, restrained; long holds, soft transitions) · **Length:** 22.5s

## Answers

- **What is it?** Cheeko is a palm-sized, screen-light AI learning device for Indian kids aged 3–10. You insert a card and stories play, or press the button and it talks back.
- **Who is it for?** Parents who want their child lost in imagination, not in a feed.
- **What sets it apart?** Physical cards, English plus 10 Indian languages, and a screen that shows no video, no feed and no ads.
- **Most impressive claim:** "Say a wish, see it drawn." Imagine draws what the child says out loud.
- **Visual hook:** the real Storytime Adventures card dropping into the real yellow device.
- **Real flow shown:** insert card → it plays. Say a wish → it's drawn.
- **Share caption:** "Less screen, more childhood."

## Angle

The magic is physical and instant: a card goes in and a story comes out. Lead with that action, then say the promise in the site's own headline.

## Sound

The soundtrack is an original piece synthesised for this video in D major at 96 BPM. It has a warm pad (Dmaj7 → Bm7 → Gmaj7 → A), plucked arpeggios, a soft sub-bass, and a light kick and shaker from scene 2 onwards. Sound effects are tuned to the key and mixed under the music: a low "thock" when the card lands, bell chimes when each picture appears, and a swell into the outro. It resolves on D at 20.0s and rings out.
