# Cheeko Lullabies / चीको की लोरियाँ

Three original goodnight songs for Cheeko, each in English and Hindi. Words written for Cheeko; music and singing made with ElevenLabs Music (commercial use needs the account to be on a paid ElevenLabs plan). Not based on any existing song.

## Folders

- **Songs**: the six tracks at bedtime volume (MP3, 48 kHz, about -21 LUFS).
- **Covers**: square album and song covers, 1500x1500, in English and Hindi.
- **Cheeko screen**: the same art at 296x240 for the Cheeko screen (pictures only, no text).
- **YouTube thumbnails**: 1280x720 versions.
- **Alternate versions**: song 1 English with longer music-only parts, for comparison.

Before release: a native Hindi speaker should check the Hindi words; make a mono version for the Cheeko speaker; keep the sleep-mode volume low and the session on an auto-off timer (see the research report in the repo, learning-center/lullabies/reports/).
